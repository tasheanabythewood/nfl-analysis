# Alt text

## 01-decision-curve

Line chart of punt rate against the win-probability edge for going for it. Left of zero, where punting is better, teams punt nearly 100 percent of the time. Right of zero the line falls slowly: at a 3 to 4 point edge for going, teams still punted 56%, and the punt rate does not drop below half until the edge passes about 4 points. A team following win probability would drop to near zero just right of zero.

## 02-asymmetry

Two horizontal bars. When going for it was better by 2 or more points of win probability, teams punted anyway 52.5% of the time across 4,085 decisions. When punting was better by the same margin, teams went for it anyway 0.3% of the time across 7,560 decisions. Random mistakes would be roughly balanced; these all lean toward punting.

## 03-season-trend

Line chart by season, 2014 to 2025. The share of clearly-go situations where teams punted fell from 71% in 2014 to 37% in 2025, with most of the drop between 2018 and 2020. A second line, for going for it when punting was better, stays below 1 percent every season. The gap between the lines is statistically significant in all 12 seasons.

## 04-field-position

Four horizontal bars, ordered from a team's own goal line to the opponent's 35. Holding the win-probability edge for going for it fixed at +3 points, the model's predicted punt rate is 97% at a team's own 1 to 20, 93% at its own 21 to 40, 84% from its own 41 to midfield, and 59% at the opponent's 49 to 35. If win probability drove the decision, all four bars would be equal.

## 05-costliest-situations

Six horizontal bars ranking situations by expected wins forfeited through punting when going for it was better by 2 or more points. Fourth and 1 between a team's own 21 and 40 is highlighted and largest, at 16.1 expected wins from 485 punts, more than twice the next situation. 4th & 1, own 41–50: 7.5 wins; 4th & 4–5, opp 49–35: 6.0 wins; 4th & 6–9, opp 49–35: 5.5 wins; 4th & 2, own 21–40: 5.1 wins; 4th & 2, own 41–50: 3.9 wins. League-wide total at this margin: 72 expected wins.
